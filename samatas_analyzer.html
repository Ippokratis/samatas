Samatas Analyzer

See your mix.

A real-time spectrum analyzer plugin. See how loud each frequency is, how it moves over time, and how your mix compares to a reference.

AU · VST3 · macOS on Apple silicon · Windows 10 and 11

What it does for you

See it clearly

10 Hz to 20 kHz, as a spectrum, a 3D waterfall or a 68-band psychoacoustic view.

Compare to a reference

Bring any track to the side chain. It shows as an orange curve over your mix, so you see where the two differ.

Measure exactly

Point at anything to read its frequency, note and cents. Check loudness in LUFS and the gain you need to hit your target.

Why Samatas

0.01 dB

Level accuracy

A steady tone reads within 0.01 dB of its true level.

6 cents

Pitch accuracy

Every reading names the note, within 6 cents of its true pitch.

40 Hz

Bass detail

High Resolution separates two notes a semitone apart, from about 40 Hz up.

Waterfall

Watch sound move in 3D

The waterfall shows the last two seconds as a 3D stack, newest at the front. See a reverb tail decay, a filter sweep, a resonance that builds.

Turn, tilt and zoom the mix in 3D. Drag the rulers to keep only the frequencies and levels you care about.

Reference

Match your mix to the records you love

Feed a reference track into the side chain. Its orange curve sits over yours, so a missing low end or a harsh upper mid stands out at a glance.

Turn on Average hold for both, and compare their balance of lows, mids and highs while the song plays.

Compare your mix with a reference

Marker

Find the note, not just the frequency

Pin one frequency, then point at another. Read the interval in semitones and cents, the harmonic number and the level difference. Tune an 808, find a resonance, check a harmonic.

Loudness

Hit your loudness target

Integrated loudness in LUFS (ITU-R BS.1770) or plain RMS, for your track and the reference. Set a target, and the Analyzer shows the gain that reaches it.

Reach a loudness target

Four views

One song, four ways to see it

The spectrum, the waterfall, the Classic Psychoacoustic view and High Resolution each show the same sound from another side.

Missed a moment? Freeze the picture and step back through the last two seconds of sound.

And everything else you need

  • Log or Linear scale
  • Mono, L / R or Mid / Side
  • Peak hold and Average hold
  • Response 140 to 5000 ms
  • Smoothing up to 1/3 octave
  • Tilt 0, 3.0 or 4.5 dB per octave
  • RMS or Peak detection
  • Presets and a saved default
  • 64 steps of undo and redo
  • Save as PNG
  • Resizable, 100% to 300%
  • Help on every control

Extra fun

Draw with sound

Turn any picture into a WAV file. Play it through the Analyzer, and the waterfall draws your picture. The page runs in your browser and uploads nothing.

Questions

How many computers can I use it on?

One license is for one person, on up to 2 Macs and 2 PCs.

Are updates free?

Every 1.x update is free for everyone who bought 1.0.

macOS says the installer can't be opened.

The installer is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS stops it the first time. Allow it once in System Settings, under Privacy & Security. The installer puts both plugins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins, for all users.

Windows says the publisher is unknown.

Click More info, then Run anyway. The setup puts the plugin in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

How do I get help?

Write to ippobour@gmail.com. I usually answer within a day or two.

Where can I learn every control?

The user manual explains every control and reading, and shows each install step with pictures, and how to remove the plugin.
