Samatas Analyzer

See how loud each frequency is, how it changes over time, and how your mix sits next to a reference. A real-time spectrum analyzer plugin for macOS and Windows.

Every control and every reading is explained in the user manual.

What it does

  • Spectrum. How loud each frequency is, from 10 Hz to 20 kHz, and up to half the sample rate when you zoom out. Log or Linear frequency scale. Mono, L / R or Mid / Side.
  • Exact readings. Move the pointer over the display to read the frequency, the note and its cents. Freeze the picture, or turn on a hold line, and the readout also shows the exact level. Smoothing changes only the drawn line, never a number. A steady tone reads within 0.01 dB of its true level and within 6 cents of its pitch.
  • Reference curve. Feed another track or a reference mix to the side chain. It is drawn as an orange curve over your own. Compare your mix with a reference.
  • Peak hold and Average hold. A heavy line at the highest level each frequency reached, or at its average level while your host plays. Turn on Average hold for your track and the reference to compare their balance of lows, mids and highs.
  • Marker. Pin one frequency, then point at another to read the interval in semitones and cents, the harmonic number and the level difference. Find the pitch of a kick.
  • Loudness. Integrated loudness in LUFS (ITU-R BS.1770) or plain RMS, for your track and the reference. Set a target, and the Analyzer shows the gain that reaches it. Reach a loudness target.
  • Freeze. Hold the picture still, then step back through the last two seconds of sound with the frame slider.
  • Classic Psychoacoustic view. 68 fixed bands that widen with frequency, in the style of the classic band analyzers used in mastering.
  • High Resolution. Separates two bass notes a semitone apart from about 40 Hz up. How it works.
  • Controls. Response from 140 ms to 5000 ms, Smooth from Off to 1/3 octave, Tilt of 0, 3.0 or 4.5 dB per octave, and Detect on RMS or Peak.
  • Presets and undo. Your own presets, a saved default, and undo and redo of up to 64 steps.
  • Save Picture. Save the spectrum or the waterfall as a PNG image.
  • Any size. Resize the window from its corner, and scale the interface from 100% to 300%.
  • Help built in. Turn on Help, and every control explains itself when you rest the pointer on it.
  • Sound untouched. The Analyzer never changes your audio. It only looks.

Four views of one song

The spectrum, the waterfall, the Classic Psychoacoustic view and High Resolution each show the same sound from another side.

Waterfall

Shows the last two seconds of sound as a 3D stack, newest at the front. Use it to see how a sound changes over time: the decay of a reverb tail, a filter sweep, a resonance that builds.

Drag to turn and tilt the stack, and zoom and move it with the mouse or a trackpad. Drag the rulers to show only the frequencies and levels you care about. Enlarge lets the stack fill the whole display. Follow one frequency over time.

Image to Sound

A small extra, just for fun. A web page turns a picture into a WAV file. Play the file through the Analyzer, and the waterfall draws your picture. The page runs in your browser and uploads nothing. Open Image to Sound.

Requirements

  • macOS. macOS 26.0 or later, on a Mac with Apple silicon. AudioUnit (AU) and VST3.
  • Windows. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. VST3.
  • Host. Any host that loads AU or VST3 effect plugins, such as Logic Pro, Ableton Live or Reaper.
  • Sample rates. Tested at 44.1, 48, 96 and 192 kHz.

Install

macOS. The installer is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so the first time you open it, macOS stops it. Allow it once in System Settings, under Privacy & Security. The installer puts both plugins in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins, for all users.

Windows. Run the setup. If Windows warns that the publisher is unknown, click More info, then Run anyway. The setup puts the plugin in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

The manual shows every step, with pictures, and how to remove the plugin.
