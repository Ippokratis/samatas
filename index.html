Samatas

Audio plugins for macOS and Windows.

I chose "Samatas" on a whim, but it means many things to many people. Choose your meaning, not war.

- Greek: σαματάς (sa-ma-TAS): a big noise, a fuss caused mainly by upset, unrest or a quarrel.

- Turkish: şamata (sha-ma-TA): noise, clatter, fuss, uproar.

- Arabic: شَمَاتَة (sha-MAA-ta): taking pleasure in someone's trouble.

- Albanian: shamatë (sha-MA-tuh): loud noise and shouting when two or more people quarrel.

- Albanian: shamatar (sha-ma-TAR): someone who makes a lot of noise and shouting; quarrelsome.

- Bulgarian: шамата (sha-MA-ta): noise, din.

- Macedonian: шамата (sha-MA-ta): noise, uproar (a regional word).

- Crimean Tatar: шамата / şamata (sha-ma-TA): noise.

- Russian: суматоха (su-ma-TO-kha): disorderly, restless running about, haste, commotion, panic.

- Polish: szamotać (sha-MO-tach): to struggle, to tug.

- Japanese: 妨げ (sa-ma-ta-geh): obstruction, hindrance.

- Japanese: 妨げる (sa-ma-ta-geh-roo): to disturb, prevent, obstruct, hinder.

- Chinese: 杀马特 (shaa-ma-tuh): a youth subculture and fashion movement known for eccentric makeup, hairstyles and clothing.

- Arabic: صَمَتَ (sa-ma-ta, with a deep "s"): to be silent, to be taciturn.

- Sanskrit: शमथ (sha-ma-tha): tranquillity, calmness, especially calm of the mind and absence of passion.

- Pali: samatha (sa-ma-tha): calm, quietude of heart.

- Sinhala: සමථ (sa-ma-tha): calm, serenity.

- Chinese: 止 (jih): calm, the Chinese word for the same practice.

- Sanskrit / Hindi: समता (sa-ma-taa): equanimity, equality, impartiality, sameness. In the classic book on theatre, it is also "smoothness", one of the merits of a play.

- Telugu: సమత (sa-ma-ta): equilibrium.

- Malay / Indonesian: semata (suh-ma-ta): only, solely, merely, purely.

- Indonesian: anak semata wayang (a-nak suh-ma-ta wa-yang): only child.

- Somali: Samatar (sa-ma-TAR): a Somali surname.

- Samata (sa-MA-ta): a feminine name in South Asia, meaning "equality" or "equanimity".

- Chad: Samata, Samata I, Samata II and Chamata (sha-MA-ta), all villages close together in Salamat.

- DR Congo: Samata, Haut-Lomami.

- Mali: Samataka, Mopti.

- Ivory Coast: Samatiguila, a town in the northwest.

- Ethiopia: Beta Samati, an ancient town found by archaeologists.

- Samoa: Samata-i-Tai and Samata-i-Uta, villages on Savai'i.

- France: Samatan, a commune in Gers.

- India: Samata Nagar, a suburb of Mumbai.

- Ancient Bengal: Samatata, an old kingdom.

- The Samata Express, a major superfast train operated by Indian Railways (South Coast Railway zone) connecting Visakhapatnam in Andhra Pradesh to Hazrat Nizamuddin (New Delhi).
